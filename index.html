The 28 letters

Tap a letter to see its four shapes and hear it. Arabic reads right to left, and most letters join to the next one, so a letter changes shape depending on where it sits in a word.

Spaced repetition

Cards you find hard come back sooner, easy ones later. A few minutes a day beats an hour once a week.

Speaking

Writing

Hear a word and type it in Arabic script. Vowel marks are optional: كتاب and كِتَاب both count.

Conversation tutor

Write in Arabic, transliteration or English. The tutor answers in simple Modern Standard Arabic, with transliteration, a translation, and a gentle correction of your last message.
